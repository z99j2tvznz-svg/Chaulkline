FIELD GUIDE / 00

FIELDWORK INSTRUCTIONS

1. Start a session and select 7, 8, or 9-foot table.

2. Take 5–10 photos with all six pockets visible. Use either the live camera or the camera-roll picker.

3. Analyze images locally; four virtual corners come from the inner cushion-nose lines. These points are NOT physical pocket-opening centres.

4. Inspect the original, overlay and rectified images; adjust virtual corner references only when needed.

5. Download or share the ZIP and upload that ZIP to your Chalkline chat.

Scope: Experimental color-segmentation and cushion-nose intersection baseline. Pocket openings, jaw tips and diamond detection have not been validated for scoring. The app does not save to a remote server.
