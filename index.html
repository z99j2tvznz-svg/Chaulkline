New in V3.13

  • Coaching test: a Reject plan button. Tap it when a plan makes no sense; it is saved with a picture of the table for the review.
  • The plan plays for position first: strokes that leave the cue ball in the zone, coming into the line. When no stroke can reach it, it says so.
  • The cue ball's path band: one line for hitting the ball thin, one for thick, see-through white between.
  • Stronger zone colours. Livelier cushion rebounds in the plan.

New in V3.12

  • Coaching test: there is always a plan. Banks, one- and two-rail kicks and safeties are planned when there is no good pot; the best plan first, the next best kind under it.
  • Percentages: each plan says its chance to make the ball and to have the next shot (a safety: the chance to hit it right and what it leaves). The zone's colours are percentages too: green 90%+, light green 80, yellow 60, red 50.

New in V3.11

  • Coaching test: the stroke is worked out in a ball-physics simulation (tip height, side spin, power, cushions, throw), tried with small mistakes, and simple strokes come first. A tough position says so.
  • The cue ball's path shows a faint band: where it goes if you cheat the pocket to the inside or the outside.
  • After a scratch: where to put the cue ball (ball in hand).
  • After each shot: how far the cue ball came off the planned line and how much further or shorter it went.
  • Menu → Power test: roll the cue ball alone at your power 2, 4, 6 and 8; the plan then shows its power in your numbers.

New in V3.10

  • Coaching test (above): small runouts with the 9 always on. Set out any balls and shoot the lowest first. Rings in each ball's colour show the pocket for the ball you're on, the next one and the one after. Green is where the cue ball should land for the next ball (red: the wrong side; dark: a ball in the way), with the cue ball's way there. After each shot it says where the cue ball landed.

New in V3.9

  • All fifteen balls: the 10 to 15 are read now, stripes seen end on too (a white cap with a coloured ring is not the cue ball). Practice reads all fifteen.
  • 8-ball: pick the game above, then Play a rack. The rack is over when the 8 goes in, or when only the cue ball is left.
  • A shot is told by your pose: down on the shot counts, standing at the table with a hand on a ball does not. Standing only keeps a move out when the app is sure.

New in V3.6

  • Drills (Menu → Drills): a cut ladder, thin cuts, a cross-side bank and a stop shot. Rings show where the balls go and the pocket; made, the next one is harder; missed, it steps back. Your drills over all sessions on this screen.
  • Free practice says what went in and how cut it was, and leaves the rest alone: a miss may have been a bank or anything.
  • In practice a cue ball picked up is not a scratch, and a row of balls along a rail is not a break.
  • Practice: set balls where you like, move them, pick them up. No racks; balls put back keep their numbers. Play a rack is for a proper rack.
  • Set the same shot up again and the review puts the tries together: all of them on one drawing, made or missed, with each try's stroke.
  • Picking up the cue ball to set a shot again is no longer called a scratch.
  • Balls put back on the table get their numbers again, even when no new rack was seen.
  • The stroke: the strike timed right on hard shots, and a small waggle is not counted as a practice stroke.
  • From V3.2: tap any shot after a session to see it from above with its pictures and clip; your stroke from the side, read on every shot.
