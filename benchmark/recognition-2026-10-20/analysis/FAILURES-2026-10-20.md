# Why Challenger B still does not complete racks (analysis only, 2026-10-20)

No training, no tracker / scorer / reader change. Inputs: the benchmark's own outputs (models of Actions run 39500020895,
replacement predictions, unchanged v0.6 scorer traces). Script: `failures.py`; per-shot tables in `results/`
(`*.failures.csv` = one row per failing real shot with every blocker; `*.failures.json` = totals and per-rack).

"Confirmed" = read directly from the scorer's trace or the reader's stored answers. "Likely" / "suspected" = inferred.

## 1. Failure breakdown (each shot judged alone; 116 real shots)

Frozen 78 failing shots, B 67, B + frozen not-a-ball 63. A shot can have several blockers.

| Blocker family (B) | Shots where present | Shots where it is the ONLY blocker |
|---|---|---|
| Uncertain identity (unnamed spot in a still look, or made ball not named before the shot) | 29 | 18 |
| Table still moving at clip end (no settled after-look) | 21 | 14 |
| Reconciliation: absence rule could not confirm a made ball | 13 | 10 |
| Shot detection (4 missed, 6 false) | 10 | 4 |
| Wrong identity (a spot named as a ball that is gone) | 10 | 3 |
| Ball missing from the after look / false pocket | 4 | 0 |

Played straight through, the same families dominate, plus 15 shots blocked by uncertainty carried from earlier shots.
For the frozen reader, uncertain identity was present in 57 failing shots (26 sole): B removed about half of it.

Unnamed spots, by why the still-table rule left them unnamed (B, confirmed from the reader answers at that spot):
"too few confident frames" 22, "number already taken by a surer spot" 24 (a second confident read of the same
number), "votes split" 1.

**The five real racks (all 0 complete):** every rack has at least 4 failing shots with different causes, so no single fix
completes a rack. Oct 16 racks: 7/11 and 6/13 shots right; mixed causes (shot detection, a wrong identity, absence rule,
one unsettled look). Far spot racks: 0/11 and 3/15; almost entirely identity at the far camera. Other side: 4/9;
5 of its 5 failures include "table still moving at clip end".

### What each family really is
- **Uncertain identity is mostly the far spot (Confirmed).** 23 of B's 67 failing shots are the far-spot session
  (26 shots, 3 right). Two specific, repeatable failures:
  1. Rack 2: the 9 sits still at the same spot for 9 clips with its stripe turned away; B reads it as the cue in every
     clip (mean p cue 0.80, 9 0.18). The real cue is read as cue more strongly, so the 9's spot is dropped as
     "number taken" every time (see `results/firstspot-shot023-after-look-crops.png`, last tile). The same pattern
     happens with a 5 read as a 3 (one ball, all 15 test pictures).
  2. Rack 1: the 8 and the 9 sit close together against the far rail; B gives each about 0.5 "not a ball", so they are
     never named (`results/firstspot-shot006-far-rail.png`).
- **"Table still moving" is mostly a test-data effect (Confirmed for 10 of 21).** 10 are Oct 18 "evening check" clips:
  fixed 20 s clips holding 2-3 shots, cut while balls were still rolling (steps of 1.3-5.5 inches per frame in the last
  second). The R0.6 recorder (Oct 19) has none. The other 11 (old-app clips) show 0.3-1.1 inches per frame in the last
  second: real slow rolling or spot jitter. Unknown which.
- **Absence-rule failures are mostly a truth-data effect (Likely).** 7 of the 10 sole cases are recorder clips whose
  starting layout was not recorded, so the system keeps 4-7 balls "unconfirmed" that were never on the table and the
  count rule cannot pass. In real play the rack inventory is known.
- **Wrong identity (10):** a spot named as a gone ball. Scorer's own reason where it can tell: "a still ball misread
  the same way every time" (far spot 3), "misread of the 9" (1); the rest cannot be told from the truth files.

## 2. Recognition weaknesses (held-out test pictures)

| Nearest other ball (radii) | Pictures | Frozen right / wrong / unsure+rejected | B right / wrong / unsure+rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 2.5 (touching) | 110 | 45 / 45 / 20 | 6 / 0 / 104 |
| 2.5 - 3.5 | 100 | 85 / 15 / 0 | 81 / 15 / 4 |
| 3.5 - 5 | 255 | 195 / 45 / 15 | 233 / 5 / 17 |
| >= 5 (isolated) | 1,118 | 911 / 91 / 116 | 1,088 / 28 / 2 |

Far spot, B by ball: cue, 1, 2, 4, 7 all right; 5 always read 3 (15/15); 3 read 7 once (15 pictures); 6 read 8 (13) or
rejected; 8 rejected or unsure 44/60; 9 unsure or rejected 62/90 (never called cue in the test pictures).

**Is the improvement just more uncertainty? No (Confirmed).** B is unsure or rejecting on 127 test pictures against the
frozen reader's 151, and right on 172 more. At product level B has fewer unresolved shots (47 against 52) as well as
more right (49 against 38). B's abstentions are concentrated where balls touch.

**Causes (Likely, not proven):**
- Touching / crowded balls: the crop contains two balls and B abstains. labels-v1 has only 8 touching examples and the
  non-ball class was learned only from app guesses the player deleted, so **missing training examples** is the most
  likely cause; image detail is adequate at the near spots (people can name these crops).
- Far spot 9 with stripe hidden: **insufficient information in that view**. The band is a sliver in a 26 px crop.
  More training data cannot fully fix a view that does not show the stripe. It needs another source of evidence
  (the ball's earlier frames, or which balls remain on the table).
- Far spot 5 -> 3 (orange vs red at about 10 px radius): image detail and colour, Unknown which. One ball only.

## 3. Detection confidence vs identity confidence (interface requirements, no tracker change)

Today one 11-way answer (cue, 1-9, not a ball) carries both "is it a ball" and "which ball", and the tracker uses it for
both (confirmed in `code/tools/tracker.js` and `reconcile.js`):
- a spot whose not-a-ball score is 0.5 or more is dropped from still looks, cannot relink a hidden track (line 288),
  and does not count toward a track being a ball (line 237);
- a weak identity (p < 0.6) WIDENS linking: a far detection may join a fast track when its number is unsure (line 162).
  This is how lowering 8/2 and 7/4 confidence changed linking and lost a shot.
- a strong contradicting read (p >= 0.8) blocks coasting onto a spot (line 175).

Requirements for reader v2's interface:
1. **Existence** (is there a ball here): from the detector + persistence over frames, not from the identity model;
   only this may create, drop or end tracks.
2. **Position**: detector / tracker only.
3. **Appearance attributes** (white, solid colour, stripe, dark): separate outputs, each with its own confidence.
4. **Identity**: a distribution over the balls still in the rack, given that it is a ball; calibrated.
5. **Rules**: unknown identity must mean "link by geometry only" with the same gates, never wider gates; only a
   confident contradiction may veto a link; identity uncertainty must never remove a detection.

Evidence that the coupling matters (post-hoc, Confirmed numbers, not pre-registered): keeping B's numbers but taking the
"not a ball" answer from the frozen reader gave 53 / 38 / 17 confidently wrong (B alone 49 / 33 / 20), and 51 / 35 / 22
on the alternate pick (B 48 / 32 / 25).

## 4. Benchmark integrity

**Directly reproduced in Actions (Confirmed):** baseline 38 / 27 / 26 / 0 of 5; frozen reader probabilities; package
unchanged; scorer files identical to v0.6 commit 7e08306; recognition metrics for all three systems; product scores from
the committed per-spot predictions; the published models' hashes and test predictions.

**Held-out handling (Confirmed from code and config):**
- Training used only Oct 12-16 player-verified labels; no Oct 17-19 session, clip or identical crop is in train or dev
  (`data/split-check.json`).
- Thresholds were not tuned: the decision rule (not a ball at 0.5, confident at 0.6) is the tracker's own, fixed in
  `config.json` before any run. Each challenger's temperature was fitted on dev only (Oct 15 + odd Oct 16 clips).
- B's training length was fixed in advance (last epoch, no early stopping on any data). The "strongest candidate" rule was
  written into `config.json` before the first test run.
- Caveats: Challenger A's held-out results were seen in a local dry run before the Actions runs (A was not changed
  afterwards). The "B + frozen not-a-ball" variant was looked at after the results, so it is a lead, not a result.
  Oct 16 is in B's train/dev data, so 116-shot product totals include 24 in-sample shots; held-out-only numbers are
  reported separately (frozen 26 / 25 / 21, B 36 / 31 / 14 of 92).

**The 7 B trainings:** seed 0 trained in 4 Actions runs (39499369643, 39500020895, 39504251615, 39504265303) and
seeds 1-4 in each of the last two. The product models are simply the first run that published its models to the branch
(39500020895, needed because artifact downloads are blocked from my side). It was not chosen for its score: its test
result (1,408 right / 48 wrong) is slightly worse than the run before it (1,404 / 42). The seed spread was computed after
and used for nothing.

**Product prediction files made outside Actions (Confirmed provenance):** `replacement-predictions/frozen30.npz` and
`d30b.npz`, made by `local/infer_footage.py` with the run 39500020895 models. `MANIFEST.json` holds the model sha256
(re-checked in Actions), the footage zip sha256 and the ffmpeg version. In Actions, at the 2,425 labelled test spots the
committed answers equal the Actions test predictions (8 differ by more than 0.01). The crops are byte-identical to the
package crops. This step can only be re-run by someone with the footage.

**Scorer limitation found (Confirmed):** the product score checks which balls are on the table and which went in, not
where each named ball is. Two balls on the table with swapped names would still score "right". Any future identity
experiment must also report per-spot accuracy on the verified frames.

## 5. Recommended next experiment: separate "is it a ball" from "which ball" (no training, no new footage)

- **Failure it targets:** real balls dropped or left unnamed because the identity model's "not a ball" share is near
  0.5 (crowded balls, far rail), and the side effects on linking. These are 4 of the 6 families above (uncertain
  identity, ball missing, false pocket, carried uncertainty).
- **Evidence:** B rejects or is unsure on 104/110 touching pictures. The far spot rack 1 failure above. The post-hoc
  existence swap gained +4 / +5 tables and -3 confidently wrong on both frame picks.
- **Experiment:** pre-registered input variants for the unchanged v0.6 tracker and scorer:
  (1) B number + frozen "not a ball" (confirm the lead);
  (2) the same, with B's number confidence taken as "given it is a ball" (B's number probabilities divided by
  1 - B's not-a-ball). This is the interface change in section 3, made at the input only.
  Nothing else changes: no tracker edit, no new rule, no training.
- **Data:** existing replacement predictions (all 11 probabilities are stored) + frozen detections; both frame picks.
- **New verified examples needed?** No for this test. Verified non-balls in the held-out sessions would still be needed
  to measure false-detection rejection properly (unmeasurable now).
- **Scoring:** unchanged scorer; all 116 shots and the 92 held-out shots alone; both 30 fps picks; per-clip list of new
  confidently wrong shots against B; shot-detection counts (to catch linking side effects); per-spot accuracy on the
  verified test frames.
- **Meaningful improvement:** at least +3 ending tables over B on the held-out 92 shots in BOTH frame picks, confidently
  wrong not above B (14 held-out / 19 alt), no rise in missed or false shots. Anything less is noise.
- **Browser cost:** none extra for the test (offline). Using B at all costs about 24.5 million multiply-adds per crop
  against about 0.11 million for the frozen reader (about 220x). Its Safari cost is unmeasured and must be measured
  before any product use.

Not recommended now: a broad recording; a whole-table assignment that uses the rack inventory to resolve the
far-spot 9-as-cue case (it fixed all 76 balls in 10 verified looks offline, but it would be a reconcile change and the
product scorer cannot see swapped names); the "table still moving" family (mostly clips cut mid-play; R0.6 already
addresses it).
