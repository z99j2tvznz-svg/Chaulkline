New in V3.9

  • All fifteen balls: the 10 to 15 are read now, stripes seen end on too (a white cap with a coloured ring is not the cue ball). Practice reads all fifteen.
  • 8-ball: pick the game above, then Play a rack. The rack is over when the 8 goes in, or when only the cue ball is left.
  • A shot is told by your pose: down on the shot counts, standing at the table with a hand on a ball does not. Standing only keeps a move out when the app is sure.

New in V3.6

  • Drills (Menu → Drills): a cut ladder, thin cuts, a cross-side bank and a stop shot. Rings show where the balls go and the pocket; made, the next one is harder; missed, it steps back. Your drills over all sessions on this screen.
  • Free practice says what went in and how cut it was, and leaves the rest alone: a miss may have been a bank or anything.
  • In practice a cue ball picked up is not a scratch, and a row of balls along a rail is not a break.
  • Practice: set balls where you like, move them, pick them up. No racks; balls put back keep their numbers. Play a rack is for a proper rack.
  • Set the same shot up again and the review puts the tries together: all of them on one drawing, made or missed, with each try's stroke.
  • Picking up the cue ball to set a shot again is no longer called a scratch.
  • Balls put back on the table get their numbers again, even when no new rack was seen.
  • The stroke: the strike timed right on hard shots, and a small waggle is not counted as a practice stroke.
  • From V3.2: tap any shot after a session to see it from above with its pictures and clip; your stroke from the side, read on every shot.
