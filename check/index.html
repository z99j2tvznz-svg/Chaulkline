Chalkline

V3 check: the new ball finder and number reader, on your phone. This page only looks and measures. It does not track shots.

What to do (about 10 minutes)

  1. Set the phone where you'd normally put it. Upright or sideways both work.
  2. Tap Start camera. The first time, it downloads about 36 MB, so use Wi-Fi.
  3. Rack a few balls. Each ball should get a teal ring with its number. Amber with a "?" means the reader is unsure. A small red cross means the reader decided it's not a ball.
  4. Phantom check: lean your arm, your hand and the cue on the table. There should be no rings on them.
  5. Measle check: put the cue ball right next to the 9, then move them around. Tap a ball to see what the reader thinks of it.
  6. Tap Speed test and wait about a minute without touching anything.
  7. Tap Save and save both files to the Chalkline test uploads folder in Google Drive.
